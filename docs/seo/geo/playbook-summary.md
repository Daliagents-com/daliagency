## Core thesis (Google-aligned)

Google's AI Optimization Guide (Search Central) frames **AEO/GEO as still SEO**.

Generative AI features (AI Overviews, AI Mode) are rooted in the **same indexing, ranking, and quality systems** as classic Search.

Tactics Google says are **not** required hacks: special "AI keyword" rewriting, content chunking for models, treating `llms.txt` as a citation lever.

Primary source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide

## Classic SEO still means

1. Crawl/index eligibility (robots, sitemap, 200s, canonical).
2. Useful pages that match search intent.
3. Clear site structure and internal links.
4. Technical quality (speed, mobile, HTTPS).
5. Trust and experience signals (who wrote this, proof, contact).

## GEO / AI visibility means

1. Be **indexable** so AI systems that ground on the web can find you.
2. Write **direct answers** under clear questions (citable blocks).
3. Build a **consistent entity** (brand name, founders, sameAs profiles).
4. Prefer **first-hand process content** over generic listicles.
5. Ignore myth playbooks that contradict primary sources.
