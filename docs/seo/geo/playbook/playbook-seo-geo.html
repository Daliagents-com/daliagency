How SEO and GEO work now

A practical brief for Dali (AI agent systems studio) on classic search and AI answer surfaces. Prefer primary Google guidance over influencer hacks.

Thesis. For Google Search, optimizing for generative AI features is still optimizing for Search. AI Overviews and related features are rooted in core ranking and quality systems. Source: Google Search Central - AI optimization guide.

1. Classic SEO today

What still decides whether pages can rank and be reused by AI features.

  1. Eligibility. Crawlable, indexable, HTTPS, useful robots/sitemap, real 200 responses.
  2. Intent match. Page answers the query better than alternatives (comparisons, how-tos, definitions).
  3. Structure. Clear H1/H2, internal links, canonical, hreflang for languages.
  4. Experience. Mobile, speed, stable layout, readable design.
  5. Trust. Who operates the site, contact, proof, first-hand process writing.

Official starting points: SEO starter guide, helpful content.

2. GEO / AI search visibility

Marketing labels: GEO (generative engine optimization), AEO (answer engine optimization). Google's position: these are not a separate ranking sport for Google Search. Quote-level idea from the guide: optimizing for generative AI search is still SEO.

What actually helps AI surfaces

  • Pages already strong enough for classic Search (index + quality).
  • Self-contained answer paragraphs under clear questions.
  • Consistent entity (brand, founders, sameAs profiles).
  • Original process knowledge (gates, audits, failure modes) - Dali's advantage.

Myths to deprioritize

  • llms.txt as a guaranteed citation lever (not a primary Google ranking instruction).
  • Special "AI-only keyword" rewriting separate from normal language.
  • Chunking content into arbitrary 150-word blocks for robots only.
  • Mass thin pages to "feed the model."

Also see Google's AI features documentation: AI features in Search.
