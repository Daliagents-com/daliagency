## EN-first (global B2B)

### LinkedIn Company
- **Why:** Default B2B entity; AI research often surfaces LinkedIn.
- **Prepare:** Logo, 1-paragraph about, website, services tags.
- **Steps:** Create company → add David + Liana → 3 posts → follow industry.
- **Post:** Process posts (gates, vibe rescue, audit offer).
- **Risk:** Empty company page looks worse than none.

### Clutch
- **Why:** Buyers shortlist agencies; reviews = trust.
- **Prepare:** Real projects, truthful categories, location.
- **Steps:** Sign up → claim → publish profile → invite real clients only.
- **Risk:** Fake reviews = ToS ban + brand damage.

### GoodFirms / DesignRush / Sortlist
- **Why:** Secondary EN lists.
- **Steps:** Complete **after** Clutch is solid.
- **Risk:** Profile sprawl with zero reviews.

### Crunchbase
- **Why:** Company graph / data providers.
- **Steps:** Basic org page if eligible; keep facts minimal and true.

### Bing Webmaster
- **Why:** Bing + some AI partners.
- **Steps:** Import GSC or DNS verify → sitemap.
