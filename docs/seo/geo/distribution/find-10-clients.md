# 10 ideas to find clients (Dali)

Each idea: channel, angle, first 3 actions, cost, lag, risk.

---

### 1) Warm LinkedIn founder network
- **Channel:** LinkedIn  
- **Angle:** Free 45-min workflow map for production agents  
- **Actions:** Company page; 20 personal notes/day; book 3 calls/week  
- **Cost:** Time (+ optional Sales Nav)  
- **Lag:** 1-3 weeks  
- **Risk:** Spammy notes kill brand  

### 2) Upwork selective gigs (already skilled)
- **Channel:** Upwork  
- **Angle:** Production agents / vibe-code rescue / fixed pilots  
- **Actions:** Refresh profile with blog proof links; 5 proposals/day quality; templates from proposal blog post  
- **Cost:** Connects + time  
- **Lag:** Days-weeks  
- **Risk:** Race-to-bottom pricing  

### 3) Vibe-code rescue outbound
- **Channel:** Manual research of AI-built sites with broken checkout/admin  
- **Angle:** Security + production hardening (honest audit offer)  
- **Actions:** List 30 targets; short non-alarmist email; link audit checklist blog  
- **Cost:** Time  
- **Lag:** 2-6 weeks  
- **Risk:** Sounds like threat if poorly worded - stay ethical  

### 4) Partner with design freelancers
- **Channel:** Personal network / Telegram / LI  
- **Angle:** They design; Dali hardens agents + production  
- **Actions:** 10 partner messages; 1-page co-sell PDF; dual CTA  
- **Cost:** Revenue share  
- **Lag:** 2-8 weeks  
- **Risk:** Bad partner quality  

### 5) Content → inbound from blog
- **Channel:** Organic SEO/GEO  
- **Angle:** Decision posts already live  
- **Actions:** GSC; interlink hubs; request index; CTA on every post  
- **Cost:** Time already invested  
- **Lag:** 1-3 months  
- **Risk:** Patience  

### 6) Local GE/AM service businesses
- **Channel:** Local intros, FB groups (careful), GBP if used  
- **Angle:** AI assistant + handoff without full rebuild  
- **Actions:** 15 local DMs; case from process not vanity metrics  
- **Cost:** Time  
- **Lag:** 2-6 weeks  
- **Risk:** Low budget clients  

### 7) Agency ops productized pilot
- **Channel:** Agencies on LI/Upwork  
- **Angle:** Recurring account ops agent pilot (Kora-pattern thinking)  
- **Actions:** Target 20 agencies; fixed pilot scope; blog proof  
- **Cost:** Time  
- **Lag:** 3-8 weeks  
- **Risk:** Scope creep  

### 8) Speaking / micro-workshops
- **Channel:** Online events, local meetups  
- **Angle:** "When not to use agents" + gates  
- **Actions:** Pitch 3 events; 30-min talk; CTA audit  
- **Cost:** Time  
- **Lag:** 4-10 weeks  
- **Risk:** Prep time  

### 9) Rebuild leads from past work
- **Channel:** Email/Telegram to past collaborators  
- **Angle:** New agent systems / visibility offer  
- **Actions:** List 40 past contacts; 5 personal messages/day; no blast  
- **Cost:** Free  
- **Lag:** 1-2 weeks  
- **Risk:** None if personal  

### 10) Productized "Audit pack" landing
- **Channel:** Site + LI ads optional later  
- **Angle:** Paid or free structured audit deliverable  
- **Actions:** One-pager scope; Calendly/email; 2 case frameworks  
- **Cost:** Time; ads optional  
- **Lag:** 2-4 weeks to first paid  
- **Risk:** Free audits that never convert
