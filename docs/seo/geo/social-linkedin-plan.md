## Does social help GEO?

**Yes, but indirectly.**

- Consistent company + founder profiles help AI systems and people resolve "who is Dali".
- sameAs links strengthen Organization schema.
- Branded search increases (people hear the name, then Google it).
- Posts rarely "rank" as pages; they **distribute** and **entity-anchor**.

**No:** posting daily memes will not put you in AI Overviews.
