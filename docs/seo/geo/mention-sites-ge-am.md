## Gaps / red flags

- Many “Georgia business directory” results are **USA Georgia** - ignore those.  
- Deep submit forms for yell.ge / ibn.ge may sit behind login or paid packages - verify before paying.  
- Telegram links can die; re-check join before relying.  
- State portals (GITA, HTI) are for real programs, not link spam.  
- Confidence: **high** for BIA, yp.com.ge, thestartup.ge, Startup Grind, TechBehemoths, I-AM.AM; **medium** for paid catalog tiers.
