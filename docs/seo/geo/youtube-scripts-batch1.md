## Video 1 - Human approval gates (6–8 min)

**Source post:** https://dali.agents.ge/blog/human-approval-gates-for-ai-agents  
**Working title:** Human approval gates for AI agents (where to put them)  
**Filename:** `dali-approval-gates-v1`

### Spoken outline

1. **Hook (20s)**  
   “If your AI agent can send email, move money, or change CRM stages without a human check, you do not have production automation. You have a delayed incident.”

2. **Direct answer (40s)**  
   Put a human approval gate before any action that is hard to reverse, high-cost if wrong, or externally visible. Do not gate pure research that only drafts internal notes.

3. **What to gate (90s)**  
   - Sends: email, messages, invoices  
   - Writes: CRM stages, refunds, permissions  
   - Money and access  
   - Public or sensitive customer replies  

4. **What not to gate (60s)**  
   Internal summaries, retrieval, sandboxed drafts that cannot leave without approval.

5. **Minimal design (120s)**  
   Name the action and blast radius.  
   Approver = role, not “anyone online”.  
   Show evidence pack: inputs, intended action, confidence, sources.  
   Log approve / reject / edit.  
   Time-box stale approvals.

6. **Failure modes (60s)**  
   Gate everything → slow chat stub.  
   Gate nothing → customer incident.  
   Vague approvers → no owner.

7. **Close (30s)**  
   “This is how Dali designs production agent systems: map workflow, place gates on irreversible steps, automate the rest inside tools you already use. Link in description for the full write-up and audit.”

## Video 2 - Rescue vibe-coded MVPs (7–9 min)

**Source post:** https://dali.agents.ge/blog/how-we-rescue-vibe-coded-mvps  
**Working title:** How to rescue a vibe-coded MVP without shaming the founder  
**Filename:** `dali-vibe-rescue-v1`

### Spoken outline

1. **Hook (20s)**  
   “Vibe coding is not the enemy. Shipping secrets, open admin, and payment paths without a stop switch is.”

2. **Direct answer (40s)**  
   We do not shame founders for shipping with AI builders. We triage severity, stop the bleeding, then decide patch vs rewrite per critical path.

3. **Triage order (2 min)**  
   Secrets in the browser.  
   Payments and webhooks.  
   Admin and auth.  
   Outbound messages.  
   Then UX polish.

4. **What good looks like (90s)**  
   Standard vs non-standard routing.  
   Knowledge base the assistant can use.  
   Session memory only where intended.  
   Secrets never in client JS.  
   Logs and a stop switch.

5. **Patch vs rewrite (90s)**  
   Not always a full rewrite.  
   Sometimes harden.  
   Sometimes isolate and rebuild only the critical path.

6. **Close (30s)**  
   “Dali hardens vibe prototypes into production systems with gates and ownership. Full process linked below. If you want a structured audit, start at dali.agents.ge/solutions.”

## Video 3 - GEO + SEO for AI agencies (6–8 min)

**Source post:** https://dali.agents.ge/blog/geo-seo-for-ai-agencies  
**Working title:** GEO and SEO for AI agencies (one system, two surfaces)  
**Filename:** `dali-geo-seo-v1`

### Spoken outline

1. **Hook (20s)**  
   “There is no secret ChatGPT ranking file that replaces indexable pages and a clear brand entity.”

2. **Direct answer (45s)**  
   For an AI agency, SEO and GEO are the same job with two surfaces: classic results and AI answers that cite the web. You still need crawlable pages, clear entities, honest expertise, and answers to buyer questions.

3. **System layers (2 min)**  
   Technical eligibility.  
   Entity clarity.  
   Decision content.  
   Citability (direct answers).  
   Distribution only when truthful.

4. **What not to do (60s)**  
   Thin keyword pages.  
   Fake case metrics.  
   llms.txt as magic.  
   One language when you promise four.

5. **Multi-language note (45s)**  
   EN / RU / GE / ARM: factual parity, shared slug families, hreflang.

6. **Close (30s)**  
   “Dali runs this system on its own site and builds visibility systems for clients. Full article linked. I’m David from Dali Agents.”
