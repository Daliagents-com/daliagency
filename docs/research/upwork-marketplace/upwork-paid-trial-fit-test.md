## Fixed trial artifact

The proposed USD 50 artifact is one executable happy-path workflow plus one failure case, using synthetic or buyer-provided non-sensitive sample data.
It must include:

1. One documented input schema.
2. One deterministic action path.
3. One human approval gate before any outward or irreversible action.
4. One structured event log.
5. One failure or retry case.
6. One short run command or operating note.
7. One acceptance check with observed output.

The artifact excludes live credentials, production deployment, autonomous outreach, real customer data, off-platform communication, voice telephony setup, new paid services, meetings, and continuing support.
If the buyer needs any excluded item, this USD 50 experiment is a mismatch.

## Economics gate

| Component | Cap |
|---|---:|
| Proposal preparation | 10 minutes |
| Scope confirmation | 10 minutes |
| Artifact build | 30 minutes |
| QA and handoff | 10 minutes |
| Total post-memo released-work time | 60 minutes |
| Platform deductions, Connect cost, and incremental tool cost | USD 10 maximum |
| Net cash | USD 40 minimum |

The platform must show the USD 50 as funded or otherwise secured before work starts.
One unpaid revision, meeting, live integration, or support request breaks the cap and kills the format.
The cap preserves the same USD 50 gross operator-hour boundary implied by the USD 100 Kora pilot's 120-minute cap.

The completed internal fit memo consumed up to 30 minutes of separately measured one-time research effort.
The first experiment can therefore consume up to 90 minutes when that memo is included.
At USD 50 gross and USD 40 net cash, that first-run evidence rate is at most USD 33.33 gross or USD 26.67 net per hour.
This is accepted only as one-time learning overhead.
Any future comparable row must reuse the method and remain inside the 60-minute post-memo cap.
