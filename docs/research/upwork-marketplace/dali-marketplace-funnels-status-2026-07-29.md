<outcome>

1. Lead Response.
2. Client Inbox.
3. Operations & Documents.
4. Internal Knowledge Assistant.

Voice Agents remains a design-partner research lane.

Content and media, staffing, broad product builds, generic orchestration, infrastructure-only work, and weak or singleton categories do not pass the current reusable-outcome gate.

</outcome>

## Commercial model

<commercial-model>

The pages do not claim that the buyer's finished product already exists.

They claim that the workflow, human boundary, pilot scope, and acceptance test have already been packaged.

The buyer is routed to:

- A direct fixed pilot when the workflow, samples, owner, access, destination, approval rules, and pass condition are clear.
- A paid workflow blueprint when value is clear but material scope or data uncertainty remains.
- Archive or disqualification when there is no stable workflow, sample set, accountable owner, or safe operating boundary.

The build starts only after the selected blueprint or pilot milestone is accepted and funded.

The selected set contains 8 explicit fixed-price rows with a $525 median, 16 numeric hourly rows with a median $25-$42.50 band, 3 hourly rows without a visible numeric band, and 5 negotiable or unspecified budgets.

These are listing anchors rather than validated willingness to pay.

Low-budget but credible opportunities route to a narrower paid blueprint, a smaller safe outcome, or archive rather than an under-scoped implementation.

</commercial-model>

## Operating boundaries

<boundaries>

No outreach was sent automatically.

The workbook is prepared for a human to review each live listing, confirm it is still open, personalize the first two lines, and submit it through the correct marketplace account.

The site uses the official Vercel Web Analytics client only on Vercel deployments.

The project is already enabled for cookie-free Vercel Web Analytics.

A validated non-personal `ref` code rewrites only the recorded analytics URL to a virtual `_ref` path.

The visible page URL, SEO metadata, and buyer-facing content remain unchanged.

Leave `Page View` as `Unknown` until the matching Vercel Analytics `_ref` path confirms the event.

The Hobby plan does not provide custom CTA events, so mailto, copy, and Upwork-checklist clicks remain unmeasured.

Reply, qualification, proposed milestone, funded milestone, and win remain the authoritative manual funnel events.

Marketplace listings prove repeated demand, not conversion.

Competitor pages validate buyer language and operating units, not Dali's close rate or universal price.

Voice is not part of Wave 1.

No page may be used as evidence that an unbuilt system is already a client case.

Do not add a broader vertical or sixth funnel until real reply, objection, interview, funded-milestone, and delivery-margin evidence supports it.

</boundaries>
