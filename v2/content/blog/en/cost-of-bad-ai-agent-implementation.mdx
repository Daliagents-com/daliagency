## Direct answer

Bad AI agent implementations cost more than the invoice: security incidents, customer trust damage, data cleanup, and teams that refuse the next automation.

## Incident costs

Wrong emails, wrong refunds, leaked secrets, compliance events.

## Operational debt

Duplicate CRM records, noisy queues, unowned bots nobody can shut off.

## Organizational cost

Shadow IT agents. Vendor lock-in. Burned stakeholders.

## Prevention

Narrow pilots, gates, acceptance tests, handoff, kill switches.

### Can you recover a bad implementation?
