## Direct answer

Brief an AI agent studio with one workflow, tools, volume, risks, and a definition of done. Unstructured 'we need AI' emails produce unusable proposals.

## Email template

Subject: Pilot brief - {workflow}. Body: context, tools, volume, pain, constraints, success, timeline, budget band optional, links to samples.

## Attachments that help

Redacted examples. Field lists. Org roles for approvers.

## What not to send

Unbounded wishlists of twenty agents. Secrets in plain text.

## What a good studio replies

Clarifying questions, pilot boundary, acceptance test draft, commercial shape.
