## Direct answer

Before you sign, ask vendors how agents fail, what requires human approval, how data is accessed, and how ownership transfers. Skip vendors who only demo happy paths.

## Process and scope questions

Which workflow is in the first pilot? What is explicitly out of scope? How do you handle non-standard cases?

## Safety and control

Where are human gates? What is the stop-switch? How are secrets stored? Can the agent send money, email, or change permissions?

## Evidence and ops

How do you evaluate quality? What is logged? Who is on-call after launch? What does the handoff package include?

## Commercial

Fixed price or open-ended? What changes the price? Who owns the code and prompts?

## FAQ

### Should vendors access production data on day one?
Prefer staged access with least privilege and a written data policy.

### Is a live demo enough diligence?
No. Ask for failure cases and exception handling.

### What if answers are vague?
Treat vagueness as a risk signal and shrink scope or walk away.
