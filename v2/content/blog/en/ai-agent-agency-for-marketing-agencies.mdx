## Direct answer

Marketing agencies get value from agents on delivery ops and client coordination, not from gimmick content spam. Hire a specialist when client-facing risk and multi-tool handoffs need gates.

## High-value agency workflows

Intake, status updates, asset checklists, reporting drafts with human publish gates, lead routing.

## Risk zones

Auto-posting without brand review. Client data mixed across tenants. Unreviewed claims in copy.

## Build vs partner

Internal ops owner + specialist studio often beats random freelancers per client.

## Pilot idea

One client communication path or one reporting draft path with approvals.

### Should agents write all client content?
No. Draft with review. Publish gates stay human.

### Can one agent serve every client?
Only with strict separation and templates. Start with one motion.
