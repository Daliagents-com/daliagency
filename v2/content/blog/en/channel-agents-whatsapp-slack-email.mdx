## Direct answer

Channel changes risk and UX. **WhatsApp** is customer-visible and regulated by platform rules. **Slack** is internal but can still leak data. **Email** often needs classify-first, not auto-send-all.

## WhatsApp / messaging apps

Template rules, opt-in, tone, escalation to humans, no unsupervised commitments.

## Slack

Internal knowledge with ACL awareness; prevent cross-channel private data leaks.

## Email

Classification and draft replies first; sending gates for external mail.

## Calendar booking

Confirm timezone, double-booking rules, and human override for VIP.

## Shared rule

Identity of the counterparty and audit trail on every outbound.
