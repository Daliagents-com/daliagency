## Direct answer

Choose a partner who can ship one production path with gates, logs, and handoff - not a partner who only presents multi-agent theater.

## Production means irreversible actions are controlled

Email, CRM writes, payments, admin, and public posts need explicit rules.

## Partner checklist

Workflow mapping skill. Tool integration honesty. Evaluation plan. Security basics. Documentation quality.

## Pilot design as the filter

If they cannot propose a fixed pilot with an acceptance test, they are not ready for production work.

## Culture fit

They should challenge bad automation ideas, not sell unlimited autonomy.

## FAQ

### Do we need ML PhDs on the vendor team?
Usually no. You need systems thinking, integrations, and ops.

### Is multi-agent architecture a must?
No. Many wins are one well-gated agent path.

### How do we test a partner quickly?
Paid audit on one workflow with written findings.
