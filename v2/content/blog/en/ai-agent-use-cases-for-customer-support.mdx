## Direct answer

For support, use agents to triage, draft grounded replies, and escalate cleanly. Keep refunds, legal promises, and account takeovers behind human approval.

## Works well

FAQ with citations. Intent triage. Draft replies from approved knowledge. Summaries for agents.

## Needs gates

Credits, cancellations, privacy requests, security account changes.

## Knowledge requirements

Approved sources beat scraping the whole internet. Version and own the corpus.

## Metrics

First response time, resolution quality, escalation rate, CSAT - with baselines.
