## Direct answer

Hire an AI agent agency with a written pilot: one workflow, one acceptance test, named tools, and human gates. Do not start from a free-floating chatbot demo.

## Write a one-page brief

Name the recurring work, tools, weekly volume, non-negotiable exceptions, and what must never be automated without review.

## Shortlist on process, not pitch decks

Ask how they map workflows, where they put gates, and what a 30-day pilot includes and excludes.

## Buy a pilot, not a program

Fixed scope and price for one path. Expand only after the acceptance test passes.

## Define ownership before kickoff

Who approves sensitive actions? Who owns the system after launch? What is documented?

## FAQ

### Do we need an RFP?
Only for formal procurement. Many teams succeed with a structured brief and two discovery calls.

### Should we hire the cheapest bid?
No. Compare cost of failure and rework, not day rates alone.

### What if we already have freelancers?
Keep them for narrow slices after architecture and gates are clear.
