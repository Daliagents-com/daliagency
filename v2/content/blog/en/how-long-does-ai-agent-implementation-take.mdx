## Direct answer

A focused production pilot is usually measured in weeks after a short discovery, not overnight. Timelines explode when scope is unclear or multiple workflows are forced into one launch.

## Typical phases

Discovery and map. Design gates and tools. Build. Replay real cases. Launch with monitoring. Iterate.

## What speeds you up

Clear owner. Stable tools. Narrow path. Existing clean data for replay.

## What slows you down

Unowned processes. Secret-ridden prototypes. Changing requirements mid-pilot. No access to real examples.

## After launch

Budget time for exception tuning. Agents are not 'set and forget' in month one.

## FAQ

### Can we ship in a weekend?
You can ship a demo. Production gates and replay take longer.

### Does multi-agent mean multi-month?
Not if the first path is narrow. Architecture theater is optional.

### When do we expand to a second workflow?
After the first acceptance test passes and ownership is clear.
