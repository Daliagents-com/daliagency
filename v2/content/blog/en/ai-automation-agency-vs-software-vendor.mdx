## Direct answer

Buy software when a product already fits your workflow with acceptable risk. Hire an agency when your process, tools, and exceptions do not match a package - and you need production integration with gates.

## Software vendor path

Faster if the product is the workflow. You still need configuration, permissions, and change management.

## Agency path

Maps your real process, connects multiple tools, designs approvals, and hardens edge cases.

## Hybrid reality

Often you buy tools and still need implementation. The mistake is expecting either side to own the full system without a brief.

## Decision table

Package fit high → vendor. Messy cross-tool process → agency. Core product IP → in-house or specialized studio.

## FAQ

### Can an agency resell a platform?
Sometimes. Demand clarity on incentives and alternatives.

### What if the vendor says their AI needs no process work?
Be skeptical. Process always leaks into exceptions.

### Which is cheaper long term?
Whichever reduces rework and incidents, not the lowest first invoice.
