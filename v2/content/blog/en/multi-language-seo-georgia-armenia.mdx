Ship factual parity across locales, use hreflang and shared slug families, adapt examples locally, avoid machine-dump pages. For regional services, entity consistency and real language quality matter more than keyword stuffing.

## What good looks like

- Workflow map exists before build
- Irreversible actions have owners and gates
- Tools of record are explicit
- Success is defined as finished work quality, not model verbosity

## What bad looks like

- Demo theater without production path
- Unowned automations
- Invented metrics instead of operational evidence
