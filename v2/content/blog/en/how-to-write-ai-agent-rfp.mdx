## Direct answer

Write an AI agent RFP around one workflow and a pilot acceptance test. Vague 'transform us with AI' RFPs produce vague, expensive proposals.

## Must-have sections

Business problem. Current process. Systems. Data classes. Security constraints. Success metrics. Pilot boundary. Timeline. Commercial model. Evaluation scorecard.

## Scorecard ideas

Process understanding. Gate design. Security. Handoff. Price clarity. Relevant experience - without requiring fake case stats.

## What to attach

Sample tickets, redacted threads, field lists, org chart for approvers.

## Common RFP failures

Fifty unrelated use cases. No owner. Mandatory proprietary platform with no justification.

## FAQ

### How long should the RFP be?
Short enough that a good vendor can answer in a week.

### Open bid or invited?
Invited shortlists usually yield better production partners.
