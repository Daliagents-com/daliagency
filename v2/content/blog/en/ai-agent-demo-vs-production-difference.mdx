## Direct answer

A demo impresses in a meeting. Production survives bad inputs, limited permissions, human gates, and Monday morning exceptions. Buy production design, not demo polish.

## Demo checklist

Scripted prompts. Broad credentials. No logs. No owner.

## Production checklist

Real tool scopes. Failure modes. Approvals. Metrics. Kill switch. Docs.

## How vendors blur the line

Hosting the demo on your logo theme without your constraints.

## Buyer test

Ask to replay last month's messy tickets, not a clean sample.
