## Direct answer

Walk away when a company sells unlimited autonomy, invents case metrics, skips approval design, or cannot explain how the system stops when wrong.

## Demo theater

Flashy chat that never touches your real tools or exception cases.

## Safety avoidance

No talk of gates, logs, least privilege, or stop-switches.

## Metric fiction

Exact revenue lift with no measurement design or access to your data.

## Lock-in without ownership

You cannot export prompts, configs, or runbooks. No training for your team.

## Scope inflation

'We automate everything' before mapping one path.

## FAQ

### Is confidence in sales a red flag?
Overconfidence without questions about your process is.

### What if they only work on their platform?
Fine if the platform fits. Bad if they refuse to discuss fit.

### Can freelancers show the same red flags?
Yes. The pattern is about discipline, not company size.
