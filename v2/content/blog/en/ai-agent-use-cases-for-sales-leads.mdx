## Direct answer

Sales agents should accelerate speed-to-lead and CRM hygiene with supervised outreach. Unrestricted autonomous email sequences are how brands burn trust.

## High-fit

Instant draft first replies. Qualification question packs. CRM field fill with validation. Meeting briefs.

## Gate hard

Discount commitments, contractual language, mass outbound to cold lists without policy.

## CRM truth

Bad automation that writes junk fields creates long-term sales debt.

## Pilot

One inbound channel into one CRM path with approval on sends if risk is high.
