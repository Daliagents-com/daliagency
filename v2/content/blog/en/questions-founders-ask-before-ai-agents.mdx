## Direct answer

Founders should ask which weekly workflow hurts most, what must never auto-run, who will own the system, and how success is measured - before buying agents.

## Strategy questions

Is this core IP or ops leverage? What happens if it fails publicly?

## Execution questions

Which workflow first? Which tools? Who approves?

## Team questions

Who is afraid of this and why? What training is needed?

## Money questions

Pilot cost vs cost of status quo. Not vanity ROI slides.

## FAQ

### Should a pre-product startup hire an agent agency?
Usually no. Ship product first unless ops pain is existential.

### What is a sane first pilot?
Inbound lead or internal ops path with clear volume.

### When to wait?
No owner, no tools access, no measurable workflow.
