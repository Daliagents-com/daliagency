## Direct answer

Expect a paid discovery or audit, then a fixed-price pilot for one workflow. Cheap unlimited 'agents everywhere' packages usually mean thin demos without gates or ownership.

## Common commercial shapes

Hourly consulting. Fixed pilot milestones. Monthly ops retainers after launch. Platform licenses on top if a product is required - prefer using tools you already pay for.

## What should be in a pilot quote

Workflow map, integrations in scope, acceptance test, gate rules, logging, handoff package, explicit exclusions.

## Hidden costs of underpriced work

Rewrites after secrets leak. No one on-call. Knowledge leaves with the contractor. CRM pollution from bad automation.

## How to compare quotes fairly

Normalize scope. Ask what happens when the agent is wrong. Ask who pays for production hardening.

## FAQ

### Is a free pilot a good sign?
Rarely for production systems. Free work often skips discovery and gates.

### Should pricing include tools?
Prefer implementing inside tools you already own unless a new tool is the product.

### When is a retainer justified?
When agents are live and need monitoring, evals, and iteration.
