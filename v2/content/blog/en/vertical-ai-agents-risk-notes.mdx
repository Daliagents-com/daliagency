## Direct answer

Vertical agents fail when they ignore **compliance and exception reality**. Start with low-blast-radius tasks: intake, status, FAQ with sources - not unsupervised legal, medical, or payment promises.

## Real estate

Lead triage OK; fair-housing and disclosure language need gates.

## Clinics

Scheduling assist OK; diagnosis/treatment advice is not a casual pilot.

## Restaurants

Standard orders OK; allergies and specials need strict confirmation paths.

## Logistics

Status explanations with system-of-record data; no invented ETAs.

## HR

Screening assist with bias review; final hiring decisions stay human.

## Returns/refunds

Policy engines + human approval above thresholds.

## Communities/Discord

Moderation assist; ban actions gated.
