## Direct answer

A **chatbot** talks. A **copilot** helps a human in-product. **RPA** clicks fixed UI scripts. A **production AI agent** takes multi-step actions with tools under permissions, gates, and logs. Buy the category that matches your risk.

## Chatbot

Conversation, FAQ, capture. Weak when it must write CRM fields or move money without a surrounding system.

## Copilot

Suggests inside a human workflow (IDE, CRM sidebar). Human remains the actor for critical clicks unless you add tools carefully.

## RPA

Brittle but predictable UI automation. Wins on stable legacy screens; loses when UIs change weekly.

## Production agent

Tool contracts, approvals, evals, stop-switch, ownership. Can include chat as a surface only.

## Buying mistake

Paying agent prices for chatbot scope - or expecting RPA stability from unconstrained LLM loops.

## FAQ

### Is Zapier AI an agent?
It can automate steps. Treat it as automation with AI assist unless you add gates, evals, and ownership like a production system.

### Can RPA and agents combine?
Yes: RPA for stubborn UIs, LLM for language and classification, with a human gate on irreversible outcomes.
