## Direct answer

Compare AI agent agencies on a shared scorecard: discovery quality, gate design, security, pilot boundary, commercial clarity, and ownership transfer. Ignore vanity awards.

## Scorecard dimensions

Process questions asked. Gate model. Security answers. Pilot template. Price transparency. Docs quality. Team who actually delivers.

## How to run bake-offs

Same brief to all. Paid discovery if needed. Compare written pilot plans, not slide aesthetics.

## Weighting

For production risk, gates and security outrank brand glitter.

## Output

Pick one partner for pilot one. Do not split one workflow across three vendors.

## FAQ

### Should price be 50% of the score?
No. Cap price weight; underpriced risk is expensive.

### What if scores tie?
Choose clearer ownership and chemistry with the workflow owner.
