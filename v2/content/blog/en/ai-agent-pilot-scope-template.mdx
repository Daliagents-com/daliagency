## Direct answer

Scope every AI agent pilot as one workflow, one primary tool path, explicit gates, a pass/fail acceptance test, and named owners. Everything else is phase two.

## Template fields

Workflow name. Trigger. Tools. Inputs. Allowed actions. Forbidden actions. Human gate points. Success metrics. Data access. Timeline. Exclusions.

## Example acceptance test shape

On 20 real historical cases: zero silent duplicates, required fields complete above a threshold, every low-confidence case creates an exception.

## How to use with vendors

Send the filled template before pricing. Reject quotes that ignore exclusions.

## Common mistakes

Three workflows in one pilot. No owner for approvals. Success = 'feels smarter' with no measure.

## FAQ

### How long should a pilot be?
Long enough to replay real cases and fix gates - often weeks, not a weekend.

### Can marketing and ops share one pilot?
Prefer separate pilots with separate acceptance tests.

### Who fills the template?
A business owner of the workflow plus whoever controls the tools.
