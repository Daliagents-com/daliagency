## Direct answer

Small businesses benefit from an AI agent agency when one recurring workflow burns time every week and can be gated safely. Skip multi-agent roadmaps that ignore ownership capacity.

## Good SMB pilots

Lead response, inbox triage, document-to-tracker, FAQ-style knowledge assist with sources.

## Bad SMB bets

Fully autonomous sales, unrestricted refunds, unattended admin changes.

## Budget reality

Prefer one fixed pilot over retainers that outpace value. Owner must stay in the loop.

## In-house vs agency for SMB

If no technical owner exists, agency must emphasize handoff simplicity.

## FAQ

### Is ChatGPT Plus enough?
For drafting, often yes. For production actions in tools, you need systems design.

### Can freelancers replace an agency for SMB?
For narrow scripts yes. For gated multi-step work, often no.

### What is the first metric?
Hours per week on the target workflow and error rate.
