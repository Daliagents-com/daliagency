## Direct answer

Buy a platform when it matches your workflow and compliance needs. Build custom (or agency-built) when your tools, rules, and exceptions are the product - and a package would force awkward workarounds.

## Buy platform when

Standard process, strong vendor security, clear exit, team will operate the product.

## Custom when

Cross-tool orchestration, special gates, embedded in existing CRM/inbox/docs, unique IP.

## Agency role in both

Configuration and change management for platforms. Full systems design for custom paths.

## Trap

Buying a platform to avoid process work. Process still shows up as exceptions.

## FAQ

### Is open source a third path?
Yes, with higher ownership cost. Budget engineering time.

### Can we start custom and move to a platform?
Sometimes. Keep configs and prompts portable.

### What does Dali prefer?
Implement inside tools you already run when possible.
