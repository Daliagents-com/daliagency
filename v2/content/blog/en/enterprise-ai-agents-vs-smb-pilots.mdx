## Direct answer

Enterprise programs optimize for governance, security review, and multi-stakeholder rollout. SMB pilots optimize for one painful workflow and fast learning. Do not copy enterprise theater into a five-person company.

## Enterprise needs

SSO, data residency, audit logs, vendor risk, change boards, phased rollout.

## SMB needs

Clear owner, fixed price, quick acceptance test, minimal ceremony.

## Shared truth

Both need gates on irreversible actions and honest measurement.

## Vendor mismatch

Enterprise-only firms overwhelm SMBs. SMB freelancers under-serve enterprise controls.

## FAQ

### Can SMB tooling work in enterprise?
Sometimes, with security review. Do not assume.

### Should enterprises still run small pilots?
Yes. Big bang multi-agent programs fail often.
