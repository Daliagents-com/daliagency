## Прямой ответ

Vibe coding slows down at payments, auth, abuse protection, complex data models, and multi-client ops. That is where product engineering, evals, and production gates take over.

## Практика

Workflow first. Язык описания → проверка money/access/outbound. Human path для non-standard.

## Хорошо / плохо

Хорошо: routing, knowledge base, session rules, secrets offline, logs.
Плохо: preview = prod, публичные tokens, нет handoff.
