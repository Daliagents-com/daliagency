## Прямой ответ

If payment or workflow webhooks are callable without auth and accept client-controlled amounts, attackers can create cheap invoices or trigger business workflows. Amounts and product identity must be server-authoritative.

## Практика

Workflow first. Язык описания → проверка money/access/outbound. Human path для non-standard.

## Хорошо / плохо

Хорошо: routing, knowledge base, session rules, secrets offline, logs.
Плохо: preview = prod, публичные tokens, нет handoff.
