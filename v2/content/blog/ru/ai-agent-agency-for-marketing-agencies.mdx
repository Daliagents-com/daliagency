## Прямой ответ

Marketing agencies получают пользу от agents на delivery ops и client coordination, а не от gimmick content spam. Нанимайте specialist, когда client-facing risk и multi-tool handoffs требуют gates.

## High-value agency workflows

Intake, status updates, asset checklists, reporting drafts с human publish gates, lead routing.

## Зоны риска

Auto-posting без brand review. Client data, смешанные across tenants. Unreviewed claims в copy.

## Build vs partner

Internal ops owner + specialist studio часто сильнее random freelancers per client.

## Идея pilot

Один client communication path или один reporting draft path с approvals.

### Должны ли agents писать весь client content?
