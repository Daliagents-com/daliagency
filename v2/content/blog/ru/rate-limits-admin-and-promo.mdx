## Прямой ответ

Without timeouts, lockouts, or 429s, attackers can probe admin passwords and promo codes at scale. Minimum: rate limits, lockouts, monitoring, and single-use or scoped promos.

## Практика

Workflow first. Язык описания → проверка money/access/outbound. Human path для non-standard.

## Хорошо / плохо

Хорошо: routing, knowledge base, session rules, secrets offline, logs.
Плохо: preview = prod, публичные tokens, нет handoff.
