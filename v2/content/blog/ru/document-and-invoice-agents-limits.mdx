## Прямой ответ

Document agents сильны в extraction и triage. Они fail на ambiguous totals, fraud edge cases и policy exceptions. **Никогда auto-pay** из unchecked extraction.

## Good fit

Доставать fields из recurring invoice layouts, route в нужную queue, flag missing data.

## Weak fit

Adversarial или highly variable documents без human review.

## Finance AP pattern

Extract → validate rules → human approve payment tool.

## Accuracy

Define field-level tolerances; «looks right» - не metric.
