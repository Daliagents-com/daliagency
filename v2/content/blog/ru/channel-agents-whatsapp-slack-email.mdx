## Прямой ответ

Канал меняет risk и UX. **WhatsApp** виден клиенту и regulated platform rules. **Slack** internal, но всё ещё может leak data. **Email** чаще нужен classify-first, а не auto-send-all.

## WhatsApp / messaging apps

Template rules, opt-in, tone, escalation to humans, no unsupervised commitments.

## Slack

Internal knowledge с ACL awareness; prevent cross-channel private data leaks.

## Email

Classification и draft replies first; sending gates for external mail.

## Calendar booking

Confirm timezone, double-booking rules и human override for VIP.

## Shared rule

Identity of the counterparty и audit trail on every outbound.
