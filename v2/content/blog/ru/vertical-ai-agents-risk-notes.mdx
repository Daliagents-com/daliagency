## Прямой ответ

Vertical agents проваливаются, когда игнорируют **compliance и exception reality**. Начинайте с задач с низким blast radius: intake, status, FAQ с sources - не unsupervised legal, medical или payment promises.

## Real estate

Lead triage OK; fair-housing и disclosure language требуют gates.

## Clinics

Scheduling assist OK; diagnosis/treatment advice - не casual pilot.

## Restaurants

Standard orders OK; allergies и specials требуют strict confirmation paths.

## Logistics

Status explanations с system-of-record data; без invented ETAs.

## HR

Screening assist с bias review; final hiring decisions остаются human.

## Returns/refunds

Policy engines + human approval выше thresholds.

## Communities/Discord

Moderation assist; ban actions gated.
