## Прямой ответ

Используйте frameworks и hosted assistants, когда они **shorten integration time** without hiding ownership. Avoid them, когда force multi-agent complexity you cannot eval or debug.

## Hosted assistants

Fast tools + threads. Still need your gates, IAM и business evals.

## LangChain-class frameworks

Useful connectors и patterns; can become abstraction mazes.

## Multi-agent frameworks

Only with clear role contracts и traces.

## n8n/Zapier

See automation comparison post - great glue, limited NLP judgment.

## Decision

Choose the thinnest layer that passes evals и your team can maintain.
