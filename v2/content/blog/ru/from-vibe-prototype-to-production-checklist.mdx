## Прямой ответ

Before production: secrets out of client, webhooks authenticated, rate limits, admin lockouts, payment amount server-side, human gates on irreversible actions, logs, backup owner, and a rewrite plan for critical paths.

## Практика

Workflow first. Язык описания → проверка money/access/outbound. Human path для non-standard.

## Хорошо / плохо

Хорошо: routing, knowledge base, session rules, secrets offline, logs.
Плохо: preview = prod, публичные tokens, нет handoff.
